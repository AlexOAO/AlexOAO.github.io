## Results

**Webai Finance launched in production and cut the time researchers spend collecting and organizing data.** I built the module alone, from requirements to launch, during my internship at KGI Financial Holding.

## Why this project

Researchers had to collect and organize data every day before they could start research; the data came from broker reports, macro data, and news. Webai Finance is the AI Market Assistant and AI Stock Assistant in the Kinance platform. The user picks a market or a stock, the AI answers from organized data, and the user can keep asking.

## My role

I owned the Webai Finance module end to end.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Requirements.** The hardest part was access design. The tool had to be open to the whole group, but investment-trust data had to stay separate. One side opens, the other closes; the access design had to do both.

**Phase 2 | PoC.** I first proved the AI could answer. I orchestrated the AI Agent flow and brought in broker reports, macro data, and news through SFTP, a secure file transfer method. Then I confirmed that the AI answers market and stock questions from that data.

**Phase 3 | Build.** I integrated the assistant into the existing user interface, so users did not need a new tool.

**Phase 4 | Launch.** Webai Finance launched in production.

**Phase 5 | Operate.** The next step is to measure the effect after launch.

## Next steps

Measure the effect after launch, for example researchers' daily data-prep time.

## Results

**Webai Finance went live, and researchers now spend less time collecting and organizing data.** I built this module on my own, from requirements to launch, during my internship at KGI Financial Holding.

## Why this project

Before researchers could start any research, they had to collect and organize broker reports, macro data and news every day. Webai Finance is the AI Market Assistant and AI Stock Assistant inside the Kinance platform. You pick a market or a stock, the AI answers from the organized data, and you can keep asking follow-up questions.

## My role

I owned the Webai Finance module and led the whole process.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Requirements.** The hardest part was access. The tool had to be open to the whole group, but investment-trust data had to be kept separate. One access design had to do both: stay open and keep that data walled off.

**Phase 2 | PoC.** I started with a small proof of concept to show the AI could actually answer. I set up the steps the AI Agent follows, and brought in broker reports, macro data and news through SFTP, a secure way to transfer files. Then I checked that the AI answered market and stock questions from that data.

**Phase 3 | Build.** I put the assistant inside the interface people already used, so nobody had to switch tools.

**Phase 4 | Launch.** Webai Finance went live.

**Phase 5 | Operate.** Next, I want to show the effect after launch in numbers.

## Why this project

Researchers spend a lot of time every day collecting and organizing broker reports, macro data, and news. Webai Finance is the AI Market Assistant and AI Stock Assistant in Kinance: the user picks a market or a stock, the AI answers from organized data, and the user can keep asking follow-up questions.

## My role

I owned the Webai Finance module and led it alone from requirements and PoC to production launch.

## What I did

- **Orchestrated the AI Agent flow**: integrated broker reports, macro data, and news through SFTP.
- **Designed access control**: open to the whole group, but investment-trust data must stay separate. The two needs pull in opposite directions; the design had to meet both.
- **Integrated with the existing UI / UX**: the AI assistant lives inside the product people already use.

## Results

Webai Finance launched in production and cut the time researchers spend collecting and organizing data.

## Next steps

Measure the effect after launch, for example researchers' daily data-prep time.

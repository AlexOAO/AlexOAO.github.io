## Results

**Webai Finance launched in production and cut the time researchers spend collecting and organizing data.** I led the module alone, from requirements to launch, during my internship at KGI Financial Holding.

## Why this project

Researchers had to collect and organize broker reports, macro data, and news every day before they could start research. Webai Finance is the AI Market Assistant and AI Stock Assistant in Kinance: the user picks a market or a stock, the AI answers from organized data, and the user can keep asking.

## My role

I owned the Webai Finance module end to end.

## What I did

**Phase 1: requirements.** The hardest part was access: the tool had to be open to the whole group, but investment-trust data had to stay separate. One need opens, the other closes; the access design had to meet both.

**Phase 2: PoC.** I orchestrated the AI Agent flow and integrated broker reports, macro data, and news through SFTP, then confirmed that the AI answers market and stock questions from that data.

**Phase 3: production launch.** I integrated the assistant into the existing UI / UX, so users did not need a new tool.

## Next steps

Measure the effect after launch, for example researchers' daily data-prep time.

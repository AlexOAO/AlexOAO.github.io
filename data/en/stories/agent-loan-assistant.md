## Why this project

**Its value is not hours saved but extra applications won, so we cannot judge it only by what it costs.** The Loan Assistant (Sales Agent) handles the first stage of the customer journey: smart customer acquisition, which aims to reach the right customers and close more deals. When customers have a question on the application platform, they often leave because nobody answers right away.

## What it does

**Who it serves and where.** It deals directly with customers and potential borrowers, chatting with them on the digital application platform and social media.

**What it can do on its own:**

- Chat with customers and explain loan products.
- Walk customers through the application step by step.
- Negotiate within its authorized limits, adjusting its approach to the customer's situation.

**What it hands to people:** Negotiations beyond its authorized limits or the interest rate threshold go to a supervisor.

**What it cannot do:** Promise that a loan will be approved.

**How people oversee it.** Within its authorized limits it works on its own, and people take over only for exceptions (human-on-the-loop).

**Architecture.** Everything runs in the cloud. Customer data is de-identified first, meaning anything that shows who the customer is gets removed before it goes to the cloud. Daily usage is also capped.

**How it is tested.** A test set of business scenarios checks how well it chats. Past real cases check whether the prices it offers go beyond its authorization.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | prototype** I created a prototype based on the customer acquisition scenario, allowing the sales team to see firsthand how to engage with customers, guide them through the application process, and negotiate prices. The prototype can be operated in practice:

- **Three channels, one assistant.** The desktop and mobile versions of the digital application platform, as well as a simulated LINE Official Account, are all powered by the same AI Loan Assistant. After accepting the quote on LINE, customers can directly proceed to the application platform to continue filling out the form.
- **The complete application process.** There are product pages and calculators for personal loans, mortgages, and auto loans, along with a four-step application process: calculation, basic information, uploading financial documents, and confirmation and submission.
- **Negotiate within the scope of authorization.** The base interest rate is determined by the preliminary review rating. The assistant takes a small step each time within the authorization; if the customer sets up automatic direct debit or a salary account, the interest rate will also be further reduced according to the rules. For requests that exceed the authorization or fall below the interest rate threshold, the assistant will create a supervisor approval form. After the supervisor approves or counters the offer in the back office, the result will automatically return to the customer's conversation and will also be included in the application form.
- **Win back customers who left midway.** If the application is stalled at a certain step for too long, the assistant will proactively ask if help is needed. This addresses the issue of "customers leaving in the middle of the application process."
- **Approval is not guaranteed.** The output guardrail will block statements such as "guaranteed approval," and the annual percentage rate (APR) must be disclosed when providing a quote. Interest rates and monthly payments are calculated by a program, not generated by a language model.
- **The data is first de-identified.** Before being sent to the cloud model, all text has any information that could identify the customer removed. There is also a daily usage limit. Once exceeded, responses will be based on predefined rules.
- **Get to the next leg of your journey.** The submitted application can be exported as an "application case package" and, together with the images of the financial documents, submitted to Smart Credit Review for import. I have actually tested it: After the Smart Credit Review System imports the data, it can recognize these images and calculate the annual income.
- **Admin** 12-page admin dashboard: Operations overview, supervisor pricing, product and negotiation authorization (modifications require approval), knowledge base, guardrail rules, de-identified records, costs and benchmarks, etc.

The prototype passed automated tests, including negotiation limits, single-round dialogue flows, and end-to-end processes from chat to application to supervisor approval.

**Trial Prototype:** [kgib-sales.xiang-wei-li.com](https://kgib-sales.xiang-wei-li.com). You can chat directly with the assistant without logging in. To apply, log in with the test account shown on the screen. All data is simulated.

**Phase 2 | Framework design.** I first worked out where the costs go, then defined its metrics:

- Business ratios: applications completed with AI help ÷ all applications; application conversion rate.
- Realization ratios: actual applications ÷ baseline applications; benefit realization.

**Phase 3 | Data collection and calculation.** I have asked the business unit for business volume. Once it arrives, I will build a three-year baseline of business volume, cost, and benefit.

**Phase 4 | Institutionalize.** After launch, track conversion and realization ratios regularly and add them to the comparison table of the four Agents.

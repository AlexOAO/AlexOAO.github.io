The measurement framework is done. Business volume is still pending from the business units, so the baseline is not set yet.

## Why this project

**Its benefit is not hours saved but extra applications won, so it cannot be judged on cost alone.** The Loan Assistant (Sales Agent) handles the first stage of the customer journey: smart customer acquisition, aimed at precise outreach and higher conversion. Customers with a question on the application platform often leave because nobody answers right away.

## What it does

**Who it serves and where.** It talks directly with customers and potential borrowers, on the digital application platform and social media, through conversation.

**What it can do on its own:**

- Talk to customers and explain loan products.
- Guide customers through the application process.
- Negotiate within its authorized limits, adjusting its approach to the customer's situation.

**What it hands to people:** Negotiations beyond its authorized limits or the interest rate threshold go to a supervisor.

**What it cannot do:** Promise a loan approval.

**How people oversee it.** It runs on its own within its authorized limits, and humans take over exceptions (human-on-the-loop).

**Architecture.** Everything runs in the cloud. Customer data is de-identified, meaning anything that identifies the customer is removed, before it goes to the cloud. There is also a daily usage cap.

**How it is tested.** A test set of business scenarios checks conversation quality. Historical cases check whether its quotes go beyond its authorization.

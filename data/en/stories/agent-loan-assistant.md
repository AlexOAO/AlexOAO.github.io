The measurement framework has been completed; the business volume is still awaiting provision from the business units, and the baseline has not yet been established.

## Why this project

The Sales Agent is responsible for the first stage of the customer journey: smart customer acquisition. The goal is to achieve precise outreach and improve conversion rates. Customers often leave the application platform when they have questions because no one answers them promptly. Its benefit lies not in the amount of working hours saved, but in the number of applications facilitated. Therefore, evaluating it should not be solely based on cost considerations.

## What it does

**Service recipients and channels.** Engaging directly with customers and potential borrowers, deploying on digital application platforms and social media, and providing services through interactive conversations.

**What it can do on its own:**

- Talk to customers and explain loan products.
- Guide customers through the application process.
- Negotiate within the scope of authorization and adjust strategies based on the customer's situation.

**What it needs to be handed over to someone:** Negotiations that exceed the authorized scope or interest rate thresholds are referred to the supervisor for handling.

**What it can't do:** Commitment approval results.

**How people supervise.** It operates autonomously within its authorized scope, with exceptions being handled by humans (human-on-the-loop).

**framework** Everything is deployed in the cloud. Customer data is de-identified before being sent to the cloud, and there is also a daily usage limit.

**How to verify it.** Test the quality of the conversation using a question bank of business scenarios, and check whether its quote exceeds the authorization using historical cases.

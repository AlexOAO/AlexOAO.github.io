## Why this project

An earnings call is when a company explains how its business is doing and what it expects next. An event trading strategy asks one question: does this event give a signal you can trade on?

## My role

I led the development of this strategy.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Hypothesis.** Earnings calls release information about a company's operations and outlook, and that may give a tradable signal.

**Phase 2 | Backtest.** Design a Taiwan stock strategy around earnings calls, then backtest it, that is, simulate trades on historical data, to judge its return and risk.

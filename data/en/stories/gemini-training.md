## Results

**The Gemini training outline is confirmed: three topics (safety, tools, prompts), with hands-on practice on use cases that each business unit brings.**

## Why this project

Handing out tools is not enough; employees also need to know how to use them. The bank encourages employees to use generative AI, but they do not know which tasks suit AI, or how to use it safely. So this course answers "how do I use it in my work", not "what features does the tool have".

## My role

I lead the course design.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Design.** Agreed the course outline with the consultant on three topics: safety, tools, and prompts.

**Phase 2 | Needs.** Collect the work scenarios each business unit wants to handle with AI, to practice hands-on in class instead of generic examples. In progress.

**Phase 3 | Delivery.** Run the course with the business units' own use cases.

**Phase 4 | Follow-up.** After the course, count two numbers: attendees, and AI use cases that attendees propose after class. The second shows whether the course reached people's work.

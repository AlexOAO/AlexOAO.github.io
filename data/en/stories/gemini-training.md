## Results

**The Gemini training outline is confirmed: three topics (safety, tools, prompts), with hands-on practice on use cases that each business unit brings.**

## Why this project

The bank encourages employees to use generative AI, but tools alone are not enough: employees do not know which tasks suit AI, or how to use it safely. So this course answers "how do I use it in my work", not "what features does the tool have".

## My role

I lead the course design.

## What I did

**Phase 1: confirm the outline.** Agreed the outline with the consultant: safety, tools, prompts.

**Phase 2: collect business use cases.** Collecting the use cases each business unit wants, for hands-on practice in class instead of generic examples. In progress.

**Phase 3: deliver the course.**

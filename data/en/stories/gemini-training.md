## Results

**The Gemini training outline is set: three topics (safety, tools, and prompts, meaning the instructions you give the AI), with hands-on practice on real work situations that each business unit brings.**

## Why this project

Handing out a tool is not enough; employees also need to know how to use it. The bank is encouraging staff to use generative AI, but people do not know which tasks suit AI, or how to use it safely. So this course answers "how can I use it in my own work", not "what features does the tool have".

## My role

I lead the course design.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Design.** I agreed the course outline with the consultant, focused on three topics: safety, tools, and prompts.

**Phase 2 | Needs.** I am collecting the work situations each business unit wants to handle with AI, so we can practice on them in class instead of on generic examples. In progress.

**Phase 3 | Delivery.** Run the course using the business units' own situations.

**Phase 4 | Follow-up.** After the course, look at two numbers: how many people attended, and how many AI use cases attendees come up with afterwards. The second number shows whether people took what they learned back to their jobs.

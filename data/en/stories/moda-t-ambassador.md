## Results

**I completed the 6th Digital Youth T-Ambassador program and helped companies adopt generative AI and AIoT.**

## Why this project

Taiwan's Ministry of Digital Affairs runs the [Digital Youth T-Ambassador program](https://moda.gov.tw/ADI/industry-counseling/digital-service/4093) (page in Chinese) with two goals: digital inclusion, and filling talent gaps in industry. Rural organizations and SMEs lack digital and AI talent, so they find it hard to adopt generative AI on their own.

## My role

A 6th-cohort Digital Youth T-Ambassador, working with the government on digital transformation.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Needs.** The program works on "local problems, talent solutions": companies and organizations bring real problems.

**Phase 2 | Design.** T-Ambassadors develop digital solutions with the companies.

**Phase 3 | Delivery.** Policy outreach and community communication with the government, and help for companies to adopt generative AI and AIoT.

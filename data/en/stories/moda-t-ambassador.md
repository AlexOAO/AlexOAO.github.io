## Why this project

Taiwan's Ministry of Digital Affairs runs the Digital Youth T-Ambassador program with two goals: digital inclusion, and filling talent gaps in industry. Rural organizations and SMEs lack digital and AI talent, so they find it hard to adopt generative AI on their own.

## My role

I was a 6th-cohort Digital Youth T-Ambassador (2025/09 to 12), working with the government on digital transformation policy.

## What I did

- **"Local problems, talent solutions"**: companies and organizations bring real problems; T-Ambassadors develop digital solutions with them and help put them in place.
- **Policy outreach**: explained digital transformation policy and available resources with the government.
- **Technology adoption**: helped companies adopt generative AI and AIoT.

## Results

Completed the 6th T-Ambassador program and helped companies adopt generative AI and AIoT.

## Results

**I completed the 6th Digital Youth T-Ambassador program and helped companies adopt generative AI and AIoT.**

## Why this project

Rural organizations and SMEs lack people with digital and AI skills, so it is hard for them to adopt generative AI on their own. That is why Taiwan's Ministry of Digital Affairs runs the [Digital Youth T-Ambassador program](https://moda.gov.tw/ADI/industry-counseling/digital-service/4093) (page in Chinese). It has two goals: digital inclusion, so everyone can use digital tools, and filling talent gaps in industry.

## My role

I was a 6th-cohort Digital Youth T-Ambassador, working with the government on digital transformation.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Needs.** The program follows "local problems, talent solutions": companies and organizations bring the real problems they face.

**Phase 2 | Design.** T-Ambassadors then work out digital solutions with the companies.

**Phase 3 | Delivery.** I helped companies adopt generative AI and AIoT, the Internet of Things with AI. I also worked with the government on policy outreach and community communication about digital transformation.

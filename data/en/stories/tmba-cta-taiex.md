## Why this project

Each TMBA algorithmic trading cohort develops strategies and presents them at a final showcase. A strategy is only taken seriously if it is repeatable and measurable: the same data must give the same result, and risk and return must be measured the same way.

## My role

I led the TAIEX futures CTA strategy.

## What I did

- Developed a TAIEX futures CTA strategy.
- Built a backtest framework so results can be reproduced.
- Optimized the strategy parameters.
- Evaluated it with return, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown: return alone hides how much risk was taken.

## Results

The strategy won 3rd place at the TMBA showcase.

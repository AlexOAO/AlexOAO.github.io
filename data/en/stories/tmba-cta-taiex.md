## Results

**My CTA strategy for TAIEX futures, which track Taiwan's main stock index, won 3rd place at the TMBA 26th algorithmic trading showcase.**

## Why this project

Each TMBA algorithmic trading cohort develops trading strategies and presents them at a final showcase. A strategy is only taken seriously if it is repeatable and measurable: running the same data twice gives the same result, and risk and return are measured with the same yardsticks.

## My role

I led the development of this strategy.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Hypothesis.** Develop a CTA strategy that trades TAIEX futures.

**Phase 2 | Backtest.** I first built the backtest framework, a tool that simulates trades on historical data, so every result can be reproduced.

**Phase 3 | Optimization.** On that framework I tuned the parameters and judged results with three measures: return, Sharpe ratio and maximum drawdown. Return alone does not show how much risk was taken. The Sharpe ratio shows how much return you get per unit of risk, and maximum drawdown shows the largest fall, so together they fill that gap.

**Phase 4 | Result.** The strategy won 3rd place at the showcase.

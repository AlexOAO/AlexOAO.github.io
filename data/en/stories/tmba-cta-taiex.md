## Results

**My TAIEX futures CTA strategy won 3rd place at the TMBA 26th algorithmic trading showcase.**

## Why this project

A strategy is only taken seriously if it is repeatable and measurable. Each TMBA algorithmic trading cohort develops strategies and presents them at a final showcase. Repeatable and measurable means the same data must give the same result, and risk and return must be measured the same way.

## My role

I led this strategy.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Hypothesis.** Develop a CTA strategy on TAIEX futures.

**Phase 2 | Backtest.** Build the backtest framework first, which simulates trades on historical data, so every result can be reproduced.

**Phase 3 | Optimization.** Tune the parameters on the framework and evaluate with three measures: return, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown. Return alone hides how much risk was taken. The Sharpe ratio shows return per unit of risk and maximum drawdown shows the largest fall, so the two fill that gap.

**Phase 4 | Result.** The strategy won 3rd place at the showcase.

## Results

**My TAIEX futures CTA strategy won 3rd place at the TMBA 26th algorithmic trading showcase.**

## Why this project

Each TMBA algorithmic trading cohort develops strategies and presents them at a final showcase. A strategy is only taken seriously if it is repeatable and measurable: the same data must give the same result, and risk and return must be measured the same way.

## My role

I led this strategy.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Hypothesis.** Develop a CTA strategy on TAIEX futures.

**Phase 2 | Backtest.** Build the backtest framework first, so every result can be reproduced.

**Phase 3 | Optimization.** Tune the parameters on the framework and evaluate with three measures: return, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown. Return alone hides how much risk was taken; the other two show it.

**Phase 4 | Result.** The strategy won 3rd place at the showcase.

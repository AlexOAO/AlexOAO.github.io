## Results

**Scanned pages in listed companies' financial statements are now read by Gemini's vision-language model (VLM) in the cloud, and this is live.** Traditional OCR only turns an image into text. A vision-language model understands the layout, tables and text together, and reads each figure along with the line item it belongs to.

## Why this project

TEJ's financial data comes from listed companies' financial statements, and some of these are scans, meaning images rather than text. What matters in a statement is the tables: each figure has to match the right line item and period. Traditional OCR only turns images into text, so it cannot read that table structure.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Requirements.** First, pin down the target, the scanned pages in listed companies' statements, and where traditional OCR falls short on tables and figures.

**Phase 2 | PoC.** Send scanned pages to Gemini in the cloud and read them with its vision-language model. The model takes in layout, tables and text together, instead of recognizing characters one by one. Validation details: to be added.

**Phase 3 | Launch.** Connect this recognition step to the financial-data processing flow; it is now live.

## Results

**Earnings call videos now turn into transcripts automatically, and then into a summary table with fixed columns.** Preparing earnings call notes takes less time as a result.

## Why this project

The research team needs to know what was said on each earnings call and what the key points were. Before, someone had to listen to each call, type it out and then summarize it, which took a long time.

## My role

A shared project. I used Whisper, a speech recognition tool, to automate how the videos are processed and summarized.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Requirements.** The research team needed more than transcripts. They needed key points they could compare and quote directly.

**Phase 2 | PoC.** First, Whisper turns earnings call audio into transcripts.

**Phase 3 | Build.** There are many calls, so I automated the whole process instead of handling files one by one.

**Phase 4 | Launch.** The system sums up each transcript's key points in a table, so researchers can compare companies and quarters directly.

## Next steps

Measure how long each call takes to prepare, before and after automation.

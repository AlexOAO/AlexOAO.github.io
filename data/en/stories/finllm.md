## Results

**I finished cleaning the data for FinLLM batch 2-1, and scoring how the model reads regulations for batch 4.** Batch 5 is being scored now, and 9 more data batches are waiting for the holding company.

## Why this project

Taiwan's FSC is leading FinLLM, a large language model (an AI model like ChatGPT) built for the financial industry; KGI takes part in cleaning its data and evaluating the model. A model learns from its data: if the data is wrong, the model learns the mistakes. In finance a mistake can also break regulations, so every item has to be handled one by one. There is no shortcut.

## My role

Contributor. I clean data, and I score how well the model understands regulatory questions.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Cleaning standard.** I cleaned, proofread, and corrected the raw data item by item, then turned the method into a standard process with fixed steps, so it can be applied to more items later.

**Phase 2 | Data cleaning.** The holding company sends one batch per week. Batch 2-1 is done, and 9 batches are still waiting for the holding company; the target is to finish all cleaning before January 2027.

**Phase 3 | Regulatory scoring.** Check whether the model understands regulatory questions correctly. Batch 4 is done; batch 5 is in progress.

**Phase 4 | Model evaluation.** Once all the cleaning and scoring is done, evaluate the model as a whole.

**Phase 5 | Delivery.** Deliver on the holding company's schedule.

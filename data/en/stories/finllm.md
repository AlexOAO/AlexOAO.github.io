## Results

**I finished data cleaning for FinLLM batch 2-1 and regulatory scoring for batch 4.** Batch 5 scoring is in progress, and 9 data batches are waiting for the holding company.

## Why this project

Taiwan's FSC leads FinLLM, a large language model for the financial industry; KGI takes part in data cleaning and model evaluation. A model learns from its data: if the data is wrong, the model learns the errors, and in finance an error can also break regulations. So every item is handled one by one; there is no shortcut.

## My role

Contributor. I clean data and score regulatory interpretation.

## What I did

**Phase 1 | Cleaning standard.** Clean, proofread, and correct the raw data item by item, and turn the method into a structured standard process to extend to more items.

**Phase 2 | Data cleaning.** The holding company sends one batch per week. Batch 2-1 is done; 9 batches remain, with all cleaning targeted before January 2027.

**Phase 3 | Regulatory scoring.** Check whether the model reads regulatory questions correctly. Batch 4 is done; batch 5 is in progress.

**Phase 4 | Model evaluation.** After all cleaning and scoring, evaluate the model.

**Phase 5 | Delivery.** Deliver on the holding company's schedule.

## Why this project

Taiwan's FSC leads FinLLM, a large language model for the financial industry. KGI takes part in data cleaning and model evaluation. A model is only as good as its training data: if the data has errors, the model learns them, and in finance an error can also break regulations.

## My role

Contributor. I clean data and score regulatory interpretation, batch by batch, on the holding company's schedule.

## What I did

- Clean, proofread, and correct the raw data item by item.
- Process one batch per week, as the holding company sends it.
- Score regulatory interpretation: check whether the model reads regulatory questions correctly.
